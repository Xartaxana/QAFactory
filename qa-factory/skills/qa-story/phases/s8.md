## 2b. Before the gate — has the ticket moved?

**Only when time actually passed** (see `s0.md` §2b): this run was resumed, or gate A's
answer came back more than 6 hours after you posted it. Neither applies — skip this section
entirely; the story has been running continuously and the snapshot still stands.

Where it does apply, run the check from `s0.md` §2b. `severity: requirements` here means the
story was tested against text that no longer stands: stop, show the diff, and let the human
decide between re-running the affected stages and delivering with that stated as a known gap
(which is a `deliver_with_risks` reason, not a footnote). `context` — new comments, a status
move, an MR link that appeared — goes in as one line of the gate message.

**Rehearse gate B before you ask it.** `gate_preflight.py --task <TICKET> --gate B --text`
dry-runs what the approval authorises — the external writes (a channel nothing can deliver
fails *after* the answer, not before it), `delivery_ready`, and the S9/finalize inputs. The
three classes of `phases/s4.md` apply: repair `factory_fixable` before asking
(`stage_done.py --stage S8 --status done` refuses while one is open), put `person_decides`
into the gate message, state a `factory_defect` in the words its `fix` prints; a person's word that passes it is recorded by you, never typed by them.
It also rehearses **the bug list itself**: a draft with no `triage_ref`, resting on a verdict
other than `APP_BUG`, on a case the critic's roll-call leaves unreviewed, or with no
`manual_steps`, is `factory_fixable` — S7 repairs it before the person is shown a list to
answer, and the person's recorded word passes it like any other block.

## 2c. Close S8 — before the gate, not after it

`story_step.py finish ... --complete` in §2 already did both halves, in the only order that
works: accept the scribe's dispatch with `stage_complete`, then record the stage. Where
`story_step` cannot run, it is those same two by hand — `controller.py --task-root
<TASK_ROOT> --operation accept` with the evidence refs and `stage_complete` (where it can
run, the door is `story_step.py finish --task <TICKET> --dispatch-id <id> --digest "..." --complete`,
the one a refused hand-written accept prints), then:

```
python "${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/scripts/stage_done.py" --task <TICKET> --stage S8 \
  --status done --role qa-doc-scribe --digest "<delivery_ready, n bug drafts, gate B next>"
```

Without the acceptance `stage_done` refuses to close a stage the controller never accepted,
and says so with the waiver that passes it.

The mirror of `s4.md`'s rule, and the controller enforces it: a gate-B decision recorded
while S8 is not `completed` is refused as *"gate before prerequisite completion"*. Closing
S8 here is also what makes the rehearsal real — `stage_done` refuses while a
`factory_fixable` finding is open, which is worth nothing once the bugs are filed and the
MR is open. Delivery, bugs and the retro all happen **after** this receipt; the digest
therefore names what is true now, never an MR nobody has opened yet. The story itself counts
as closed only after what the person decides at gate B is carried out (section 4, "The orders of gate B").

## 3. GATE B — one message, this exact order

Read `test-report.md`. The message is the short form; the report is the long one. Send
one message:

1. **The verdict in the report's words** — its opening paragraph (bugs, proven / not
   proven / waiting / deferred) and the `delivery_ready: yes | after_checks | no` line

   and last *Factory defects — nothing is wanted from you*); `ledger.json`'s `blocked_by` is
   the machine answer to "whose is it": when it reads exactly `["factory"]`, say so in your
   own first sentence — the product is not what is unready, and nothing in that list is the
   person's to repair — unless the ledger has a factory reason with code
   `COVERAGE_EVIDENCE_GAP`: then say instead that those behaviours stay unproven.
2. **The traceability table** — `traceability.py --task <TICKET> --render` verbatim, every
   row: one per requirement × case, sorted by R then TC, results from the closed human list.

3. **Product bugs · Left with you · Skipped by the plan · Not proven** — the report's four sections as written.

   `qa-factory/artifacts/<TICKET>/test-report.md`.

4. **A decision per gap** — one line per row of the ledger that is not `passed_green`, and

   > fix now / verify on another environment / defer with a reminder / accept the risk.

## 4. Record, then execute verbatim

One call builds the packet and records it — the operation is `gate-decision`, not `decision`
(`decision` takes a hand-written `--input`, which is the one thing nobody should compose).
Record only words that approve and ask no change. Words that ask for one (a fix, a test, the
report) are not recorded: rework the stage first (`rework`), then ask again:

```
python "${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/scripts/controller.py" --task-root <TASK_ROOT> \
  --operation gate-decision --gate B --words-file <file> \
  --drafts "<the refs of the drafts they chose, comma-separated, or omit for none>" \
  --bugs ordered|not_ordered --mr open|do_not_open|not_offered [--risks-file <file>]
```

`--risks-file <file>` holds their reason to deliver with known gaps (see below); with it use `--mr open`, never
`do_not_open` or `not_offered` (the controller refuses the pair; the verdict `no` offers the MR only through this word).

The decision gives **orders**, each with its executor (`references/stages.json`, S8 `gate_orders`):
`bugs` is carried out by `qa-bug-reporter`, `mr` by the delivery step. You turn their words into the closed
flags (that reading is your judgment, never a phrase list in code): `--drafts` with the refs they chose orders
`bugs`; `--bugs ordered` orders it with no drafts chosen (a quarantine debt file, `DEBT-n.md`, is the reporter's
too, so a quarantine without bugs is `--bugs ordered`); `--mr open` when they said to open the MR,
`--mr do_not_open` when they said no MR is needed, `--mr not_offered` when the verdict was `no` and no MR was offered. If you
leave a flag out, the controller's defaults stand: `--bugs` omitted orders the reporter only when `--drafts`
names refs, and `--bugs not_ordered` together with `--drafts` is refused (two doors: drop `--drafts`, or record `--bugs ordered`), and `--mr` omitted
orders the MR. Say so when you rely on a default, and correct it afterwards with `settle_order`
(below) rather than leaving a wrong order standing.

`<file>`, in this and the other commands of this phase, holds their words exactly as they
were given, written with the host's file tool to `<TASK_ROOT>/state/words/<name>.txt` (name it
`gateB-<YYYYMMDD-HHMMSS>.txt`: a later answer is a new file, never an overwrite, and no file is
deleted; never a secret-file name such as `credentials.txt`): a command line cuts a
multi-line text at the first line break (a three-paragraph gate-B answer was once recorded as
its first paragraph), so words never go inline. Read the answer of the gate-decision command
whole: never pipe it through `cut`, `head` or `tail`. The controller records `Q<n>: <letter>` lines, each on its own line; words in another form go through `question_register.py --answer` with the hash `--show` prints. Quote the answer's `questions`: never tell the person an answer was recorded unless `questions.recorded` or `already` lists it; at gate B quote `questions.plan_not_changed` (a letter recorded here does not change the plan: a change after gate A is `rework`, the person's call).

**The verdict is computed again after their answers.** A stand-gap answer (`<case id>: <word>`)
can change it, so before the bug reporter or the MR the driver runs `check_traceability.py`,
`ledger.py` and `check_delivery.py` once more, re-renders the report's code-owned blocks
(`report_facts.py --write`, which adds "Added after gate B" under Left with you) and passes
`report_facts.py --check`; the delivery is told the new word. Where the skill path runs it by
hand, the same three scripts and the same `--write` / `--check` come before anything goes out.
No verdict-gated act reads a word, a head or a Left-with-you section older than the answers.

**When the factory cannot go on to the delivery, the person gets a closed question with prices, never a promise.** The report does
not match the gate B answers: `no delivery` ends the delivery with nothing sent (the delivery verdict stays as the report has it) and calls off only the
MR order: the `bugs` order stays, so the reporter still files the drafts the person chose (the driver records it as
`settle_order --order mr --outcome cancelled --words-file <their words>`), `stop` holds the question; the gap is the factory's own and is never put as a choice to own it. The delivery check could not answer
(it stopped or printed nothing readable): one defect of the factory is recorded, and `retry` runs the check again at once (a
factory command, nothing more from the person), `no delivery` and `stop` as above. The delivery step asks for a word: `go on`
(one more session of the conductor; the story line is the verdict word the check printed, and on "ready once you finish the
checks" the person line is the checks left with them in Left with you), `retry` (the person repaired what was undone; one more
session of the conductor, delivery waits), `no delivery`, `stop`. `go on` and `retry` are two records; any other words go to the
conductor, which offers these back.

`--drafts` takes the immutable original proposal refs behind the numbers they answered with
("file 1 and 3") and records them as `selected_drafts`; without them every bug proposal
reads `provenance_invalid` in the report and the person's choice is lost. The controller
derives the scope and revisions, records the journal and refreshes `pipeline.json`; never
write either projection or a replacement draft ref by hand.

A bug found by reading the code (the packet marks it, and asks `file` / `not needed` / `prove ... by a run`): `file` -> its ref goes in `--drafts` (`selected_drafts`); `not needed` -> left out of `--drafts`; `prove by a run` -> read the requirement's plan row and run `controller.py --task-root <TASK_ROOT> --operation rework --stage <S3 when no plan row executes it | S5 when the case has no test | S6 when the test exists and did not run> --role <that stage's role> --cases <case> --words-file <the answer file>`; a refused rework is a finding, not a silent drop.

A merged bug (the packet's "One bug or several"): `split` (these are different bugs) and `merge` (these are one bug) are words that ask a change and are never recorded as a decision: run `controller.py --task-root <TASK_ROOT> --operation rework --stage S7 --role qa-failure-analyst --cases <the cases of the draft> --cause question_answer --words-file <the answer file>`, then ask gate B again. The analyst writes one draft per group the person named, each `possibly_same_cause` back to the others; on a `merge` the one draft's `mechanism.basis` carries `person {dispatch, quote}` - this rework's dispatch id and the person's own words about these bugs; when drafts of a `split` share a `defect_location`, it writes `distinct_defect` carrying the person's words. The driver road reaches the same rework: `record_by_code` records no words that are not an approval.

**An answer that matches no option.** Re-ask once, and only when the missing information is
critical for the testing itself. Anything else is a remark, recorded and carried, never
argued with:
`waiver.py --task <T> --remark --stage S8 --words-file <file>`.

**Words about the retro are not a choice.** The retro is always the full one: words that ask to skip it or to keep only the numbers are kept as a remark (`waiver.py --task <TICKET> --remark --stage S9 --words-file <file>`), which the gate B packet and the MR's open items carry; they are never recorded as a mode (`stage_done.py` refuses `retro_mode=numbers|skip`).

A no-trace run records the delivery as nothing outward and ends there; its MR order is called off with the delivery step's own
record: `settle_order --order mr --outcome cancelled --reason-code no_trace --ref "<the one-line record>"`.

**`deliver_with_risks` needs a reason.** Without a reason it is not recorded and the MR is not opened —
"deliver anyway" with no stated reason is not a decision, it is a shrug (ask once). The gate B decision
records it and writes the journal line itself: the reason goes in a file, `--risks-file <file>`, with
`--mr open`; a whole line of their words `deliver with known gaps: <reason>` is read by the controller
the same way (words in any other form are your reading: ask, then use the flag). The answer's `risks`
says what stands: `risks.journal: false` means the line was not written, and its `line` names the door.
When the decision is already recorded and only the line is missing (a refused delivery command names
this), write it by file, as its own call:

```
python "${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/scripts/log_append.py" --event deliver_with_risks \
  --task <TICKET> --reason-file <file>
```

`stage_guard` reads the journal line, never the gate B record: without it every delivery command stays
blocked. Never record a new gate B decision for this: it orders the reporter and the MR again.

Then, in the order the human gave:

- **Bugs** — the `bugs` order. `controller.py ... --operation next` answers `execute_gate_b` with
  the order, its executor and the chosen drafts; dispatch `qa-bug-reporter` with the chosen drafts and the
  gate B decision line that authorized each: `story_step.py start --task <TICKET> --stage S8 --role
  qa-bug-reporter`, then `bind`, then `finish` (S8 is already `completed`, so the receipt is the accept
  alone; that accept settles the order as done, nothing else does - a word never marks `bugs` done). If the
  person says to leave the drafts as drafts, the order is called off with their words: `release` of the

  --words-file <file>`. New bugs after the order was settled are a new gate B decision (`gate-decision --gate B`
  again with `--drafts` and `--bugs ordered --mr not_offered`; without `--mr` the MR is ordered anew). With a judge
  configured `start` hands it `work/judge-bugs.json` — each draft's possible duplicate and a
  priority from the damage line; a duplicate is a proposal to the person, never a reason to
  drop a draft they chose. Drafts the human called "not a bug" are not filed and not deleted.
  **Skill road only - after the filing, before the MR** (on the driver road the driver runs both itself after the reporter, and you

  bug changes the traceability appendix, the code-owned blocks and the MR description (it must not say "chosen at gate B, not filed
  yet" about a bug that is filed); the code renders them again, and S8 is
  never reopened for a block of code, the scribe's door being only a missing report, damaged markers and the scribe's own items.
- **MR** — the `mr` order. Not wanted (`--mr do_not_open` or `not_offered` at the decision) = no order.
  If the person says "no MR" after the decision, call the order off with their words: `controller.py
  --task-root <TASK_ROOT> --operation settle_order --order mr --outcome cancelled --words-file <file>`.
  Opened: `controller.py --task-root <TASK_ROOT> --operation settle_order --order mr --outcome done --ref
  "<MR url or id>"` (the order is done only with the record of the MR that was opened, at most 2000
  characters). Through the VCS adapter named in `config.adapters.vcs` (`references/adapters-read.md`);
  title and message from `config.git_conventions`. The delivery gate knows the commands of

  allowlist:**
  1. test code — the files in `automation-manifest.json`'s `tests[]`;
  2. compact QA records — first run `delivery_manifest.py --task-root qa-factory/artifacts/<TICKET> --output qa-factory/artifacts/<TICKET>/state/delivery-manifest.json`; stage every exact path in its `files[]` plus the manifest, never the whole `work/` directory; for a local story also qa-factory/local-tickets/<TICKET>.md. This includes the stable human documents, cases/links and compact ledgers/reports while excluding `evidence/`, `work/evidence/`, logs, XML/TRX, screenshots and media (evidence is git-ignored and stays on the tester's machine: no screenshot is ever added to the repository or the MR, and the report and the MR text carry no link or path to one; the report only says per case whether screenshots were provided). `retro.md` is added by the authorized post-S9 update below. `finalize_task.py` rebuilds this manifest in place as its last step, so run `delivery_manifest.py` again after the story's last write (acceptance, a waiver, the ledger) and stage the rebuilt file.      is still waiting.

## Exit

S8 was closed in §2c, before the gate — do not close it a second time. What is left here is
the record of what the delivery actually did: the bug reporter's accept settles the `bugs` order, the
`settle_order` calls above settle the `mr` order, and an MR or external write that went out is already
journalled by the script that performed it.

**Then go on to S9 in this window** (print its card: `story_step.py card --stage S9`), but only after
`controller.py --operation next` answers `closed`: while the gate-decision answer's `next_action` is
`execute_gate_b`, the orders are not all executed or called off, and S9 is refused. Delivery belongs to this window — the human said "deliver"
and it happens now — and so does the retro: once the last order is settled `next_action` is `closed`
with `stage: S9`, and the retro starts here. Only code ends the window instead: `next_action` is
`stop` (`pipeline.stop_after: "gate_B"`: say `<TICKET> stops after Gate B (pipeline.stop_after)`
and end; before ending, give the stand's config back: `python "${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/scripts/story_step.py" config-restore --task <TICKET>`, and tell the person its `line` if it has one), or the answer's `window` line asks for a change — quote it after the delivery above,
never before it.

> `<TICKET>` — delivered. `<MR url, or what was filed>`.

A run that is interrupted here loses nothing: S9 is `pending` in `pipeline.json`, the audit
finds a story that ends without it, and `qa-doctor` names it as the next action.

## Halts of this phase

| Condition | Action |
|---|---|
| `qa-doc-scribe ... cannot assemble gate B` | What the script reported. The gate never opens on a hand-built ledger. |
| Ledger row count does not match plan rows plus tests | Halt naming the missing or extra rows. No hand-patching. |
| A guard denial on a delivery command | The guard's message verbatim: the verdict is neither `yes` nor `after_checks` and no `deliver_with_risks` is recorded. It never halts the bug reporter: that role opens on the recorded gate B decision. |
| `deliver with known gaps` given without a reason | Ask for the reason, once. Nothing is executed until it is stated. |
| `stage_done` prints a `STORY BUDGET:` line and exits non-zero | The story reached the ceiling its own project set. Show the line verbatim, stop, and ask whether to go on; their word continues it (`waiver.py --block "story budget"`). It is not a fault of the work, and it is never presented as one (person-owned: money). |
